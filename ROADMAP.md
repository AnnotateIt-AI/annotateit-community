# Roadmap

This roadmap shows the current direction of AnnotateIt AI at a high level.

- Priorities may change as we learn more from usage and from reports in this repository.
- Inclusion here does not guarantee that an item will be delivered.
- No dates are promised, and nothing here should be treated as a commitment or a contract.

Released changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Now

- Train/validation/test dataset organization
- Improve dataset validation and quality workflows
- Expand documentation and dataset format examples

## Next

- Oriented bounding boxes
- Broader import and export compatibility
- Improve cross-platform project workflows

## Exploring

Directions we are investigating. These are the least certain items on this page.

- Video object tracking
- Additional local model architectures
- Command-line tooling
- Additional dataset quality assistance

Have a view on any of this? Open a [feature request](https://github.com/AnnotateIt-AI/annotateit-community/issues/new?template=feature.yml) describing the workflow you need. Concrete use cases influence priority more than votes.
